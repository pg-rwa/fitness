# Peqo — Product Vision

> *Your AI fitness companion that talks, tracks, and trains with you.*

Peqo isn't a fitness app. It's a fitness **companion** — a character that lives on your phone, knows your body, understands your life, and coaches you through every rep, meal, and recovery day. Every fitness app today is a clipboard. Peqo is a brain.

---

## The Mascot

Peqo is a small, expressive AI character that lives on screen:
- Bounces and flexes when you PR
- Sleeps when you should be sleeping
- Looks concerned when you skip days
- Celebrates streaks with animations
- Has seasonal outfits and unlockable skins
- Evolves visually as your fitness level grows (Peqo gets buffer with you)

You don't just use Peqo. You don't want to let Peqo down.

---

## Revolutionary Features

### 1. Voice-First Gym Brain
Walk into the gym. Say *"Hey Peqo, I'm here."*

Peqo analyzes your voice tone, checks last night's sleep data, and responds:

> *"You sound tired. Sleep was 5.2 hours. I've swapped heavy deadlifts to moderate rack pulls and added extra mobility. Let's have a solid session, not a broken one."*

During your workout, just talk: *"Done"*, *"That was hard"*, *"Skip this one"* — Peqo logs everything by voice. No touching your phone with sweaty hands. No typing. Just conversation.

### 2. Ghost Mode — Race Your Past Self
Like racing games where you race your ghost car. Peqo overlays your best previous performance as a real-time target:

> *"Last Thursday you hit 8 reps at 80kg with RPE 7. You're at rep 6 now... push for 9."*

Beat your ghost? Confetti. New ghost recorded. A never-ending competition with yourself.

### 3. Machine Handshake (NFC/Bluetooth)
Walk up to a gym machine. Peqo detects it via NFC tag or Bluetooth beacon:
- Loads YOUR seat height, pin position, cable angle
- Displays your last weight/reps on this exact machine
- Staff see machine usage analytics

> *"Lat pulldown. Last time: 60kg × 10. Today's target: 62.5kg × 8. Seat position 4."*

The gym becomes **your** gym. Every machine remembers you.

### 4. Injury Oracle — Predictive, Not Reactive
Peqo tracks patterns no human would notice:

> *"You've increased pressing volume 43% over 2 weeks while sleep dropped 18%. Shoulder injury probability is elevated. I'm inserting rotator cuff prehab and capping bench at 85% today."*

It catches injuries **before** they happen by cross-referencing volume, sleep, recovery, form trends, and historical injury data.

### 5. Menu Whisperer
Point your camera at **any restaurant menu**. Peqo instantly:
- Highlights dishes that fit your macros in green
- Suggests modifications: *"Get the steak bowl, swap rice for extra veggies, sauce on the side — saves 380 cal, adds 12g protein"*
- Remembers what you ordered here last time
- Learns your taste preferences over time

### 6. Dream Morph — See Your Future Self
Upload a selfie. Peqo generates a realistic AI visualization of what you'll look like at your goal physique. Not a filter — a genuine predictive morph based on:
- Current body composition
- Training history and trajectory
- Rate of change from measurements

Updates monthly with real progress photos side-by-side. Motivation that's visceral and personal.

### 7. Negotiation Training — Plans That Breathe
No rigid "Week 3 Day 2" programs. Every session starts with a conversation:

> **You:** "I only have 25 minutes and my knees hurt"
> **Peqo:** "Upper body push/pull superset, 6 exercises, 25 min. I'll cover legs tomorrow with knee-friendly movements. Deal?"
> **You:** "Deal"

The plan is alive. It adapts to your reality in real-time, every single day.

### 8. Life-Aware Scheduling
Peqo reads your calendar (with permission):
- Meeting at 3pm? Workout auto-adjusts to finish by 2:40 with cooldown
- Flight tomorrow? Pre-loads a hotel bodyweight workout
- Kid's birthday party Saturday? Peqo moves leg day and accounts for cake macros
- Period tracking integrated? Adjusts training intensity across the cycle
- Vacation mode? Maintenance plan auto-activates

### 9. Gym Crowd Sense
Peqo detects gym busyness via Bluetooth device density:

> *"Bench area is packed. I've rerouted your chest workout to start with cable flyes and dumbbell press instead. We'll circle back to bench in ~12 minutes."*

Real-time workout rerouting around gym traffic. No more standing around waiting.

### 10. The Memory — Conversational Health Record
Peqo remembers **everything** — not as data points, but as conversation:

> *"Three weeks ago you mentioned your left hip felt tight after squats. Your squat depth has dropped 2 inches since then. Want me to add hip mobility work, or should we talk to a physio?"*

No fitness app has conversational health memory. Peqo is your fitness diary that reads itself.

### 11. Accountability Stakes
Set a goal. Put money on it.

> *"I'll work out 4x/week for 8 weeks"*
> Stake: $200. Miss a week? $25 goes to a charity of your choice. Hit all 8? Money stays + Peqo achievement unlocked.

Real stakes. Real commitment.

### 12. Trainer Clone
Have a real-life trainer? Peqo learns their style over 4-6 sessions:
- Their coaching cues ("chest up, drive through heels")
- Their programming philosophy (high volume vs. intensity)
- Their warmup preferences

On days you train alone, Peqo coaches you **in their style**. Your trainer is always with you.

### 13. Social Pulse — Real-Time Competitive Moments
Not likes and comments. Live competitive energy:

> *"Your friend Alex just benched 100kg across town. You're 5kg away. Want to attempt it today?"*

> *"3 people in your gym just finished workouts. You're the only one who hasn't started. Let's go."*

### 14. Auto-Generated Highlight Reels
Hit a PR? Peqo auto-creates a 15-second shareable clip:
- Stats overlaid (weight, reps, improvement %)
- Before/after comparison frames
- Celebration animation with Peqo mascot
- Branded watermark

One tap to Instagram/TikTok. Built-in viral marketing.

### 15. Biometric Passport
One QR code = your complete fitness identity:
- Injuries, allergies, goals, current program
- Body composition history
- Nutrition targets and preferences
- Training preferences and equipment settings

Show it to a new trainer, physio, or doctor. They scan it and know everything instantly.
